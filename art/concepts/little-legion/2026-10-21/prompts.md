# Little Legion — exploración visual 01

Conceptos generados con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Son propuestas de arte para tienda y menú; no son capturas del juego ni funciones implementadas. El juego no se modifica en esta exploración.

Cada propuesta conserva la misma marca, lema, tres razas conceptuales y cuatro acciones del menú para comparar el estilo. «Colecciones» es una propuesta futura.

## 01 · Minimalista y delicado

Archivo previsto: `01-minimalista.png`.

### Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: polished art-direction board for a mixed-reality tabletop real-time strategy game, including store key art and a main-menu visual mockup. Brand: LITTLE LEGION. These are original exploratory concepts for future visual direction, NOT screenshots of implemented features.
Canvas: wide landscape 16:9, high resolution. A beautifully designed two-panel presentation, left approximately 57% for store cover illustration, right approximately 43% for a simple main menu. A small restrained header above both panels and generous margins; composition must feel authored, not a generic template.
Invariant content across styles: small armies come alive on a compact raised tabletop battlefield with a miniature base, resource crystals and a handful of infantry. Three future cosmetic themes are represented by one prominent miniature hero each: Pharaoh Cats (an Egyptian cat soldier with a turquoise-and-gold striped headpiece), Gummy Brigade (a red translucent gummy bear soldier), Cosmic Crew (a small violet alien with dark almond eyes in a teal spacesuit). No humans, no headset required. Keep creatures charming and clearly distinct. The battlefield must read as strategy with multiple units and a base, not three unrelated mascot portraits.
Left panel: compelling store key art, readable logo "LITTLE LEGION" and the EXACT tagline "Pequeños ejércitos. Grandes batallas." Preserve Spanish accents and punctuation. Leave comfortable breathing room around lettering. Art is the main focus, not explanatory prose.
Right panel: practical, elegant MAIN MENU mockup in the same visual world, readable high contrast typography, consistent alignment and spacious selectable targets suitable for a controller ray. Clearly prominent primary button "Jugar", followed by smaller equally aligned "Multijugador", "Colecciones", "Ajustes". No other buttons, no currencies, no prices, no timers, no battle pass, no browser/device frame. Use a small low-detail miniature scene behind or beside the menu without competing with labels. Keep the layout minimal even when the art is exuberant.
Additional exact small labels: above left panel "ARTE DE TIENDA", above right panel "MENÚ PRINCIPAL"; at the bottom edge "CONCEPTO VISUAL".
No existing brands, no watermarks, no extraneous paragraphs, no unrequested slogan. Spell LITTLE LEGION correctly everywhere.

Art direction: MINIMALIST AND DELICATE. Matte porcelain-like 3D collectible figures, very simplified rounded geometry, subtle tactile surfaces, soft daylight, warm ivory background, muted sage and sand with restrained turquoise accents. Lots of negative space. A tiny sandstone Egyptian outpost, translucent candy-red bear restrained in saturation, a softly violet alien; smooth sculpted toy forms with almost no surface noise. The tabletop is pale wood or ivory, beautifully calm. Refined contemporary typography, modest rounded sans-serif wordmark, fine separators. Right menu is an off-white card with quiet dark text, one muted teal primary button, secondary actions as simple understated rows. Clearly adult premium design, gentle, airy and confident, never sterile, not a cluttered mobile game. Header text exactly "01 / MINIMALISTA".
```
