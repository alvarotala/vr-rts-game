## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE polished concept presentation for LITTLE LEGION, with store key art and a matching main-menu mockup. This is a refinement anchored in the CURRENT GAME assets supplied, not a redesign of the characters. Wide landscape 16:9.
Input image roles, in exact order:
1. Reference screenshot, Forest Watch overview: use this actual map language: a broad flat green rectangular battlefield, very shallow squared raised central terrain, sparse clumps and perimeter rows of simple faceted conifer trees, open combat lanes, pale blue resource clusters. Do not transform it into a fantasy island.
2. Reference screenshot, industrial base closeup: preserve the recognizable cyan BLUE BLOCK-BUILT stepped headquarters with dark inset, exactly three yellow upright roof bars; small blue cuboid workers with yellow top panels; pale cyan faceted mineral clusters and a grey polygonal purple gas vent.
3. Reference screenshot, biological base closeup: preserve the pink/mauve FACETED organic lobed headquarters, ivory spikes, red team accents, little low-to-the-ground insect workers with thin ivory legs. These are the current biological creatures, not humanoid cartoon aliens.
4. Reference screenshot, current unit lineup: use the current contrast of simple blue rectangular mechanical infantry with dark legs and box shields, low twin-barrel artillery, and pink/red faceted multi-legged biological creatures. Keep the recognizable simple construction. Do not add faces or clothing.
5. Reference screenshot, current main menu: keep the slate/navy and turquoise interface identity, simple rectangles, readable white text, generous control size. Restyle its presentation delicately while retaining the current available actions.

Primary request: make an attractive, believable next-step art direction from these ACTUAL models and maps, borrowing the clear hierarchy, quiet elegance and tactile miniature presentation of a premium minimal menu. The user explicitly wants it to still look approximately like their existing game.
Composition: a neat two-panel board, left 60% store illustration, right 40% main-menu exploration, thin warm off-white margin and subtle dividers. Small header exactly "05 / GEOMETRÍA ACTUAL". Above the left panel "ARTE DE TIENDA", above right "MENÚ PRINCIPAL". Small footer exactly "CONCEPTO VISUAL".
LEFT: three-quarter/isometric near-tabletop view of a cropped section of Forest Watch with enough of the rectangular outline and original shallow central terrace to recognize the screenshot layout. Cyan block base and squad on near-left, small pink biological hive with a small opposing squad further right. Resources near bases; sparse angular pines toward the perimeter. Armies remain SMALL relative to the map, not giant foreground mascots. Keep terrain clean and open, not decorated densely. Use a closer camera than the full-map screenshot so existing simple units can be appreciated; objects remain the same design rather than acquiring extra detail. Upper area holds a modern strong but understated geometric sans-serif wordmark "LITTLE LEGION"; below it exactly "Pequeños ejércitos. Grandes batallas." Preserve accents. No crown or medieval ornament. Allow clean negative space around title.
RIGHT: a simple flat, slightly rounded DARK SLATE main menu panel over a soft blurred view of the SAME current low-poly battlefield. Small clean title "LITTLE LEGION", tagline if room. Main full-width turquoise button "Nueva partida". Below it a compact two-column arrangement: "Multijugador LAN", "Guías", "Configuración", "Historial". These are the ONLY menu actions. Thin quiet icons if any, no explanatory body text. One clear primary action. Big enough to select with a VR controller ray. Keep strong contrast and practical label sizing; thin border and very restrained shadow, no glass reflections over text. Interface calmer and more refined than the original, not a mobile-game shop.
Style: intentionally SIMPLE LOW-POLY GEOMETRIC 3D, visible broad flat-shaded polygon faces, large solid color regions, box primitives for industrial units, pointed low-poly shapes for biological units. Soft directional lighting and contact shadows, gentle ambient occlusion, matte materials, restrained teal/ochre/mauve palette drawn from the actual game. Attractive promotional composition with the SAME low-detail assets; don't upgrade to elaborate sculpting, realistic textures or Pixar-like character anatomy.
Critical exclusions: NO Egyptian cats, NO gummy bears, NO grey/lilac humanoid aliens, NO new future skins; NO giant heroes, NO expressive eyes or humanoid faces; NO castles, medieval walls, rivers, waterfalls, stone bridges, immense cliffs, lush detailed grass, photoreal fur or glossy gelatin; NO cars/tanks absent from unit reference; NO loot shop, currencies or invented functionality. No loading screen, no browser or device frame. Not a collage of the screenshots. Build a new cohesive composition faithfully using their geometry and current visual language. This should look like the existing prototype art directed and lit more carefully, not a completely different game.
```
