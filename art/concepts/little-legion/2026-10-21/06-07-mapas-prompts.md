```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a landscape 16:9 concept board for LITTLE LEGION containing store key art on the left and a matching main menu on the right.
Input references, in exact order:
1. APPROVED FOREST CONCEPT: use ONLY its presentation system, layout, typography, polished low-poly rendering, scale of the small units, and menu hierarchy. Replace its forest environment with the target map specified below. Maintain a coherent series with this board.
2. CURRENT TARGET MAP overview: this is the source of truth for the target environment, terrain structure, landmarks and open combat lanes.
3. CURRENT TARGET MAP industrial closeup: accurately retain the cyan cuboid stepped headquarters with dark top inset and three yellow roof bars, small blue box-shaped workers with yellow top panels, pale cyan polygonal crystals and grey polygonal vents with lavender vapor.
4. CURRENT TARGET MAP biological closeup: accurately retain the pink/mauve faceted lobed hive with ivory spikes and red accents, small multi-legged insect workers with ivory legs.
5. Current unit lineup: use those simple blue geometric mechanical soldiers, low paired-barrel artillery and red/pink low-poly biological creatures. Keep recognizably close to this asset complexity, no faces, no elaborate character redesign.

Presentation: follow reference 1 closely: clean warm off-white margins and thin separators; top small header for this variant; left 60% promotional art, right 40% menu mockup; panel labels exactly "ARTE DE TIENDA" and "MENÚ PRINCIPAL", bottom exactly "CONCEPTO VISUAL".
LEFT: closer isometric/three-quarter view of the target map, still reading as a large open tabletop strategy battlefield, small blue base and readable small squad toward the near-left, biological base and small opposing squad toward the upper-right. Only a handful of units and a few clusters of resources, broad clean combat lanes. Show the map's recognizable structure rather than burying it in props. Use gentle contact shadows and tasteful directional lighting to elevate the ACTUAL simple assets. At the top, match reference 1's modern geometric wordmark exactly "LITTLE LEGION" with LITTLE in ivory and LEGION in cyan. Beneath it exactly "Pequeños ejércitos. Grandes batallas.", maintaining the accents.
RIGHT: same clean dark slate rounded menu as reference 1 over an intentionally blurred view of THIS target map. Title "LITTLE LEGION". The main full-width turquoise action reads "Nueva partida". Below, two rows of two smaller buttons: "Multijugador LAN", "Guías", "Configuración", "Historial". These are the only actions. Small quiet icons, comfortable padding and high-contrast lettering, simple surfaces, one clear primary action. No redesign into a neon control room or ornamental fantasy interface.
Style: honest simple flat-shaded low-poly geometric 3D, broad visible polygon faces, cuboid industrial meshes, faceted lobes and thin pointed legs for biological meshes. Cohesive tasteful presentation. The approved forest concept gives the exact target level of polish. Not photorealistic, not Pixar mascots.
Strict constraints: no cats, gummy bears or future humanoid aliens; no giant heroes, expressive eyes, castles, new tanks, cars, spaceships, dense city skylines, currencies, shops, prices or invented features. No water, rivers, waterfalls, giant cliffs or forest trees unless the supplied target map contains them. Do not reuse the forest landscape from reference 1. No browser or device frame. No unrelated text or watermark. This is a NEW composition using the reference models and map language, not a collage of screenshot rectangles.
Target map: DESERT, matching references 2–4. Header exactly "06 / DESIERTO". Warm ochre/orange sand plane with subtle repeating ripples, very shallow squared layered plateaus/ramp edges, sparse stylized banded sandstone outcrops with small green branching cacti nearby. Match the sparse layout in the screenshot; no large dunes that hide units, no canyons, no pyramids, no palms or lush vegetation. Keep industrial units cyan and yellow and biological units mauve and red. Show the characteristic stepped terrain as an important shape in the composition. Warm late-afternoon directional light, gentle long shadows, softly dusty air with clear readability. Sandstone and cacti remain as simple geometry, not highly textured rocks. The main-menu background shows blurred sand, a low sandstone formation and a few pale blue crystals. The palette pairs warm sand with the established cyan/slate interface.
```
